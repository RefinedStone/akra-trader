# Intelligence research lane

- Direction id: `intelligence-research-lane`

## Goal

Make LLM-assisted research traceable and replayable without weakening deterministic sandbox or live
promotion guarantees.

## Current status on May 11, 2026

- The lane still stops at `DecisionEnginePort`, template strategy shapes, and trace-capable
  envelopes.

## Immediate gaps

- prompt version registry
- raw trace storage
- replay harness and evaluation workflow
- deterministic fallback or human-review enforcement
